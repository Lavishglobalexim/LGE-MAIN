ABOUT

Lavish Global EXIM, headquartered in Erode, Tamil Nadu (the historic spice and textile hub of South India), is an international trading, sourcing, and export powerhouse connecting India's rich artisanal heritage and agrarian abundance to international buyers worldwide.

Strategically located within direct proximity to three premier South Indian sea gateways — Tuticorin (V.O. Chidambaranar), Chennai, and Cochin Ports — we provide end-to-end logistics with full Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, and SGS/Custom inspection certifications.

✓Direct sourcing from certified farms, master craft clusters & premier export mills
✓Comprehensive lab testing, moisture check & Fumigation certifications
✓Flexible container loads (FCL / LCL), competitive FOB & CIF contracts
