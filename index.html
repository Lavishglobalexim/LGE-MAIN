About Us

About Lavish Global EXIM

Lavish Global EXIM, headquartered in Erode, Tamil Nadu (the historic spice and textile hub of South India), is an international trading and export powerhouse connecting India's rich artisanal heritage and agrarian abundance to international buyers worldwide.

Strategically located within direct proximity to three premier South Indian sea gateways — Tuticorin (V.O. Chidambaranar), Chennai, and Cochin Ports — we provide end-to-end logistics with full Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, and SGS/Custom inspection certifications.

✓Direct sourcing from certified farms, master craft clusters & premier export mills
✓Comprehensive lab testing, moisture check & Fumigation certifications
✓Export custom packaging, private label branding, and barcode compliance
✓Flexible container loads (FCL / LCL), competitive FOB & CIF contracts

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the most trusted and distinguished Indian exporter, setting international benchmarks in agrarian purity, artisanal authenticity, and maritime supply chain excellence across global trade corridors.

Our Mission

To empower South Indian farmers, weavers, and heritage artisans by delivering their finest export-grade commodities directly to global commercial buyers through uncompromising quality assurance, transparent Incoterms, and punctilious port execution.

Ports of Loading (POL)

South Indian Seaports & Gateways

Tuticorin Port (V.O.C)

INTUT
Primary Gateway

V.O. Chidambaranar Port — The closest deep-water maritime terminal from Erode, offering seamless sea transit to the Gulf, Red Sea, Europe, and USA with minimal transshipment delays.

  • Road Distance from Erode~330 km via NH383
  • Vessel OperationsFCL · LCL · Bulk & Reefer
  • Primary Trade LanesMiddle East, Mediterranean, Europe, US East Coast
  • Transit Time (Gulf / Dubai)~4–6 Days Direct
  • Transit Time (Rotterdam / Antwerp)~18–22 Days

Chennai Port (CPA) / Ennore

INMAA
Secondary Hub

India’s second-largest container port with premier terminal infrastructure (DP World & PSA), handling high-volume consumer goods, textiles, and bulk shipments to Far East and Americas.

  • Road Distance from Erode~380 km via NH44/NH48
  • Vessel OperationsFCL · LCL · RoRo · Air Cargo (MAA)
  • Primary Trade LanesFar East Asia, Australia, Americas, Africa
  • Transit Time (Singapore / Port Klang)~5–7 Days
  • Transit Time (Los Angeles / New York)~26–32 Days

Cochin Port (Vallarpadam)

INCOK
Western Gateway

International Container Transshipment Terminal (ICTT) at Vallarpadam, Kerala. Strategically situated right on the main international east-west maritime trade route.

  • Road Distance from Erode~290 km via NH544
  • Vessel OperationsFCL · High-Speed Feeders · Air Freight
  • Primary Trade LanesEurope, UK, Gulf, Scandinavian Ports
  • Transit Time (Colombo Transshipment)~1–2 Days
  • Transit Time (London Gateway / Felixstowe)~16–20 Days
