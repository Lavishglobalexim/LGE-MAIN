Our Comprehensive Export & Sourcing Services

Delivering high-precision maritime forwarding, regulatory clearance, and verified vendor procurement tailored to international trade standards.

Ocean Freight Forwarding (FCL & LCL)

Direct volume contracts with leading global carriers (Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd, CMA CGM, ONE). We guarantee vessel allocation, optimized routing, and competitive sea freight from major South Indian sea terminals.

  • Full Container Load (20ft, 40ft & 40ft High Cube containers)
  • Less-than-Container Load (LCL) consolidated cargo groupage
  • Reefer & temperature-controlled units for fresh spices
  • Regular direct sailings from Tuticorin, Chennai & Cochin

Air Cargo & Sample Logistics

Time-critical express air freight solutions for urgent consignments, high-value artisanal products, and rapid pre-shipment buyer sample approvals to destinations worldwide.

  • Direct airline uplifts via Coimbatore (CJB), Chennai (MAA) & Cochin (COK)
  • Same-day dispatch for quality test samples via DHL / FedEx
  • IATA compliant handling, security screening & airline palletization
  • Full traceability with milestone airway bill (AWB) updates

Customs Clearance & Documentation (CHA)

Punctilious customs brokerage desks operating directly at Tuticorin, Chennai, and Cochin ICDs. We eliminate demurrage and clearance hurdles with 100% compliant documentation.

  • Electronic Shipping Bill (SB) filing under DGFT & Indian Customs
  • Phytosanitary inspection, analysis and health certificates
  • ISPM-15 Heat Treatment & Methyl Bromide fumigation certifications
  • Certificate of Origin (COO), SAFTA, Indo-UAE CEPA & GSP desk

Direct South India Sourcing

As South India's premier sourcing company, we bridge global buyers with verified farmer collectives in Erode, Salem, Kerala, Guntur, and artisan master woodcraft clusters.

  • Zero middlemen markups — direct mill, quarry, and farm gate pricing
  • Independent laboratory batch assays (Moisture, Curcumin, Piperine)
  • Ethical trade verification, fair artisan wages, and traceability
  • Flexible contract farming & seasonal supply commitments

Export Packaging & Private Labeling (OEM)

Engineered packaging that survives harsh maritime transits while delivering supermarket-ready retail presentation, custom buyer branding, and barcode compliance.

  • Food-grade multi-layer nitrogen-flushed & vacuum pouches
  • 25kg / 50kg PP woven sacks with inner poly-liners & traditional jute bags
  • ISPM-15 certified heat-treated wooden pallets with stretch-wrap
  • Custom OEM branding, private labels, bilingual prints, and UPC/EAN barcodes

Warehousing & Multimodal Drayage

Centrally located dry and humidity-controlled warehousing in Erode, integrated with dedicated inland container depot (ICD) drayage to all major sea gateways.

  • Clean, pest-controlled storage facilities with 24/7 CCTV surveillance
  • In-house multi-commodity container stuffing and weight verification
  • GPS-tracked heavy trailer fleet for fast port transit
  • Buffer stock management for recurring overseas buyer contracts

International Incoterms 2020 Matrix

We structure our sales contracts according to your organization's logistical, freight, and risk allocation preferences.

CIF Cost, Insurance & Freight

Most Popular: We cover export packing, inland haulage, customs, ocean freight, and marine cargo insurance up to your foreign discharge seaport.

FOB Free On Board

We deliver cargo across vessel rails at Tuticorin, Chennai, or Cochin port. Buyer charters the vessel and arranges marine insurance.

CFR Cost & Freight

We handle export clearance and ocean freight shipping to your destination discharge port. Buyer arranges marine cargo insurance separately.

EXW Ex Works (Factory)

Cargo packed and made available at our Erode facility. Buyer’s freight forwarder arranges pickup, inland transportation, and customs clearance.

DAP Delivered At Place

We arrange ocean shipping and inland trucking straight to your warehouse door in the destination country. Buyer handles import customs duties & local taxes.

DDP Delivered Duty Paid

Full turn-key door-to-door fulfillment where we handle freight, customs clearance, import clearance, and applicable duties.
