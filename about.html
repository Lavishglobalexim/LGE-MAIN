Corporate Profile

South India's Trusted Export & Sourcing Partner

Lavish Global EXIM, headquartered in Erode, Tamil Nadu (the historic turmeric and textile capital of South India), is an international trading, sourcing, and export powerhouse connecting India's rich artisanal heritage and agrarian abundance to commercial buyers worldwide.

Strategically located within direct proximity to three premier South Indian sea gateways — Tuticorin (V.O. Chidambaranar), Chennai, and Cochin Ports — we provide end-to-end logistics with full Phytosanitary, Certificate of Origin, and SGS/Custom inspection certifications.

✓Direct sourcing from certified farms, master craft clusters & premier export mills
✓Comprehensive lab testing, moisture check & Fumigation certifications
✓Export custom packaging, private label branding, and barcode compliance
✓Flexible container loads (FCL / LCL), competitive FOB & CIF contracts

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as South India's most trusted and distinguished sourcing company and exporter, setting international benchmarks in agrarian purity, artisanal authenticity, and maritime supply chain excellence across global trade corridors.

Our Mission

To empower South Indian farmers, weavers, and heritage artisans by delivering their finest export-grade commodities directly to global commercial buyers through uncompromising quality assurance, transparent Incoterms, and punctilious port execution.
