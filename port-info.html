Our Primary Indian Sea Gateways

Located in Tamil Nadu, Lavish Global EXIM maintains dedicated container stuffing, customs clearance, and forwarding operations across three major Indian seaports.

Primary Gateway INTUT

V.O. Chidambaranar Port (Tuticorin)

📍 Gulf of Mannar, Tamil Nadu | Approx. 330 km from Erode Hub

Our primary loading gateway. Tuticorin is an all-weather, deep-water port with round-the-clock pilotage, offering the lowest inland drayage transit time from our Erode manufacturing facilities.

  • Direct weekly feeder connections to Colombo & Jebel Ali hubs
  • Fast-track DGFT customs clearance & EIC Phytosanitary desks
  • Dedicated reefer points for temperature-sensitive cargo
  • Ideal for Spices, Textiles, Woodcrafts, and Heavy Freight
⏱️ Transit to Jebel Ali: 8–10 Days ⏱️ Transit to Singapore: 6–8 Days
East Coast Hub INMAA

Chennai Port (Madras)

📍 Bay of Bengal, Tamil Nadu | Direct Rail & Expressway Connectivity

The largest container terminal in South India. Features direct mainline vessel calls to Europe, the United States, Far East, and Australasia without intermediate feeder transshipment.

  • Direct mainline sailings via Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd
  • Automated terminal gates and bonded container freight stations (CFS)
  • Full break-bulk and hazardous cargo compliance capacity
  • Ideal for Large FCL Volume Contracts & Time-Critical Cargo
⏱️ Transit to Rotterdam/Hamburg: 20–24 Days ⏱️ Transit to US East Coast: 24–28 Days
Transshipment Terminal INCOK

Cochin Port (Vallarpadam ICTT)

📍 Arabian Sea, Kerala | Direct West Coast Access

India's first international container transshipment hub. Located directly on the prime East-West international ocean trade corridor, providing the fastest sailing times to the Middle East and Africa.

  • Direct proximity to international maritime trunk routes
  • Advanced cold-chain warehousing & fumigation stations
  • Specialized spice board laboratory and organic certifying desks
  • Optimal for Spices, Agro-commodities, and Middle East CIF contracts
⏱️ Transit to Middle East (Gulf): 5–7 Days ⏱️ Transit to Mediterranean: 14–16 Days
Commercial Trade Options

International Incoterms 2020 Guide

We contract under all standard ICC Incoterms to match your procurement, freight liability, and import structure.

CIF Cost, Insurance & Freight

Most Popular: We cover export packing, inland haulage, export customs, ocean freight, and marine cargo insurance up to your foreign discharge seaport.

FOB Free On Board

We deliver cargo across the vessel rail at Tuticorin, Chennai, or Cochin port. Buyer books the ocean freight vessel and arranges marine insurance.

CFR Cost & Freight

We handle all export clearance and ocean freight shipping to your destination discharge port. Buyer arranges marine cargo insurance separately.

EXW Ex Works (Factory)

Cargo packed and made available at our Erode facility. Buyer’s freight forwarder arranges pickup, inland transportation, and customs clearance.

DAP Delivered At Place

We arrange ocean shipping and inland trucking straight to your warehouse door in the destination country. Buyer handles import customs duties & local taxes.

DDP Delivered Duty Paid

Full turn-key door-to-door fulfillment where we handle freight, customs clearance, import clearance, and applicable duties.
